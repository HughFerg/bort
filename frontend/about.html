About

What is Flanderize?

A visual search engine for seasons 1–12 of The Simpsons. Describe what you're looking for and it finds the frame.

How does it work?

Every frame runs through CLIP — a neural network that translates images into text. Your search gets the same treatment, then it finds the closest match.

Why does it sometimes return weird results?

CLIP understands what things look like, not who they are. Character names don't always work, describe what you're seeing instead.

Why only the first twelve seasons?

I personally can't keep watching past that point.
